TX archives contain original CERN-OHL-W-2.0 RTL and generated native netlists,
Apache-2.0 methods/tests and CC-BY-4.0 measured reports. Runtime, cell library,
LEF and model inputs are recorded by exact identity rather than redistributed.
Native cell functional checks, preplacement timing and subsequent placed
measurements have separate scopes. No full PHY, main-chip or manufacturing
acceptance is established by these development captures.

finite-taps.tar.xz retains selected Magic source before/after patches and its
UC Regents notice (LicenseRef-Magic-1985), and IHP technology/model notices
under Apache-2.0. Finite device/grid controls do not qualify a complete bank
when actual ESD devices or other native connectivity remain missing.

Divider v3/v4/v5 compact evidence preserves failed predecessors, original
CERN-OHL-W-2.0 circuits and Apache-2.0 methods. Full raw waves and the thermal
pole/streaming native capsules have immutable external release references.
The padded-bank native failure archive preserves unchanged project masks,
source-bound native output and the failed complete graph audit.
