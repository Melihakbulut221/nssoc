The unchanged padded analog bank passes a complete intrinsic-device graph
comparison with finite body contacts. Parasitic capacitor values, routing

training, ordered-set and CRC owners. They do not establish complete PCS,
LTSSM, PMA or a trained PCIe Link. The physical TX function proof is a
binary Boolean state/output proof, separate from timing acceptance.

late-write and process ownership guards. The two producer scripts and
active thermal experiment's pinned inputs remain unchanged.
