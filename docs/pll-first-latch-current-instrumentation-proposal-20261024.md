The authenticated existing-capture comparison did not establish a cause. All318 transitions in anomalous intervals and all198 matched-control transitions follow a same-direction data crossing and show the defined preceding voltage competition. Native current semantics are not yet qualified. At the actual ngspice source commit, vbic.c exposes ic/ib/ie/is and qbe/qbc, but vbicload.c adds some charge derivatives into the stored current states before vbicask.c forms its answers. Those are not simply conduction-only currents, and separately handled outer-charge branches prevent assuming complete macro-terminal KCL. The exact frozen HBT model also differs from the current upstream file. The next prerequisite is cloud-only exact-model/native-hierarchy/accessor readback, with source-backed current semantics; it must reject missing or misleading queries before any transient. No ideal shunt, guessed current, zero fallback or parameter change is proposed.
