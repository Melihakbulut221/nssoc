# Shorten physical public-pin extensions

The selectively widened supply model38901377382 completed the unchanged34ns loaded study with78516rows and no native diagnostics, but remains rejected: output minimum38.648mV/28samples below100mV, CMOS137.804mV/64samples below150mV, final90.575mV/576samples below150mV, and12HBT operating-limit failures. The compact baseline was better (output96.130mV/1failure) but also unaccepted. Neither model is promoted to a completed PHY.

The compact generator previously extended every public Metal5 conductor to x=0 regardless of its first real contact. This makes single-contact avdd and din travel roughly250um and210um before reaching the actual device. The next physical candidate places each4um public pin beside the first real conductor access and retains the complete rail between real contacts, plus3um enclosure at either end. Internal signal rails, all device/material/placement/finite-contact geometry, all M3/M4 widths and2x2via stacks remain those of the better compact baseline. No ideal internal supply or signal alias is introduced.

This is an actual GDS/LEF pin-location change. Future mainchip interconnect must connect those actual new ports and include its own parasitics; the loaded component bench still has the explicitly stated external ideal boundaries. New component DRC560categories, raw100+aggregate deep/flatLVS and all7faults are mandatory before fresh terminal binding/RC/composition and original loaded electrical gates. The body-short negative control moves into the actual overlapping avss/sub rail span so it remains a real physical short after port relocation. No source waveform, time phase or numerical gate changes.
