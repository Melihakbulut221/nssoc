Experimental native-resistor TX characterization and resistor-unit physical checks. Both original failed 2mA and corrected 1.5mA matrices are preserved with exact waveforms and source snapshots. Raw waves are stored as their original .dat bytes; original gzip hashes remain in producer records, with raw hashes independently verified before archiving. Numbered lossless archive segments are each below the GitHub single-file limit. No PCIe compliance, full PHY layout, current-lifetime or manufacturing qualification.

External R3_CMC/PDK model code, OSDI and tool binaries are not redistributed; exact dependencies and hashes are recorded.
