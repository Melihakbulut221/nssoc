# LCRC word selector: measured timing regression
The functionally proved candidate is not adopted into canonical RTL. Run [39896567224](https://github.com/Melihakbulut221/nssoc/actions/runs/39896567224) completed the native three-corner comparison of the exact mapped netlists from the earlier proof.
At the slow corner, the index launch cone improves from -3.777191162 ns to +1.218624830 ns; the new one-hot launch cone has +0.221452862 ns slack. However, the worst register-to-register setup worsens from -6.768252850 ns to -7.244259834 ns. Typical and fast worst setup also regress. Fast hold worsens from -0.064122111 ns to -0.066402882 ns.
These are fixed 4 ns, 0.25 ns uncertainty, 5% early/late derate diagnostic results with ideal clock and zero wire RC. They are not routed full-chip measurements or qualified timing closure. The cell count reduction and positive selection-cone slack do not override the measured worst-path regression.
Next repair must inspect the worst native launch/capture paths and CRC combinational cone, preserve exact packet behavior, and measure the full reliable-packet path before adoption. Current routing uses the older immutable packet-level SoC netlist.
