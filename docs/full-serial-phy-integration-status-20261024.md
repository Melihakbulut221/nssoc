## Actual clock-generation prerequisite

The native mux tree requires 1 clock at 4 GHz, 2 at 2 GHz, 4 at 1 GHz, 8 at 500 MHz and 16 at 250 MHz. Adjacent mux phases within each level differ by 125 ps, as defined in [run_cloud_tx_mux4.tree_plan](https://github.com/Melihakbulut221/nssoc/blob/665e65c0220648c2e9d4859f5aaa2eaf2ec4729d/scripts/run_cloud_tx_mux4.py). A single divided clock fanned out to every pin does not implement this requirement.

The existing divider exports only slave QP/QN. A new isolated native candidate exposes actual master MP/MN and slave QP/QN through real metal while preserving 19 HBT + 20 RPPD + 4 MIM + 12 finite taps (55 devices). Physical run 40079962143 at 60b6cc9bf263a3fc2e08aa434bff4292a6df1143 must pass before electrical qualification. Its fixed MIM asymmetry is not accepted as deterministic phase reset. A physical reset/seed/synchronization design must establish phase ownership; no .ic-only initialization, UIC or post-hoc phase fitting can substitute for it.

An external PLL-output stimulus may qualify an isolated clock component only. It cannot qualify the final PLL-connected parent or full PHY.
