# Direct clock metal candidate
The sixteen additional Via2 cuts improved extracted clock access resistance by only 1.62-1.94 percent. Full loaded test 41540256258 remained rejected. This candidate instead places eight real M3/Via3/M4/Via4/M5/TopVia1/TopMetal1 stacks and connects each original four-terminal clock net by 4um-wide TopMetal1.
Every M3 landing must be wholly inside the original extracted same-net conductor. All touched layers are checked for foreign-net clearance and existing cut separation. All original 13 conductor unions must match the authenticated native wire extraction. The router checks complete geometry for every candidate path; it cannot accept a path through an obstacle. Missing any terminal or route rejects the entire candidate. Serialized layout must contain exactly the declared additive seven-layer delta and no removal or other-layer change.
Dimensions derive from unchanged IHP SG13G2 rules commit 5e6d592e4002946a4616f798c357f0f3c06cf3b6, tech-default SHA256 07930b016cdffe14c2cb82b47fbec193643347607db6d44aa1abfdece056cd2f. The copied rule constants retain the upstream Apache-2.0 license, https://github.com/IHP-GmbH/IHP-Open-PDK/blob/5e6d592e4002946a4616f798c357f0f3c06cf3b6/LICENSE .
The original devices, schematic and failed measurements are retained. Geometry collision checks are not full DRC/LVS, electromigration, or electrical signoff. Required next gates: full 560-category DRC and deep/flat transistor LVS with original fault controls; canonical identity/material reconciliation; fresh distributed RC; unchanged full loaded simulation. No resistance was idealized or removed. A 650-device matching layout, full closed-loop PHY and mainchip final timing remain open.
All native payloads are acquired and generated only on GitHub-hosted runners, with permanent complete success/failure release publication.

First actual run41545004371 rejected the complete candidate: all eight stacks were admissible, but two forced collector-to-collector routes could not detour past clipped obstacle boundaries. No GDS was accepted. This revision uses full intersecting obstacle polygons and builds a connected tree from admissible links, retaining every rejected edge. A cloud geometry regression requires successful routing around an obstacle extending beyond the former100um search window. Complete original 9 controls remain, plus that regression.
